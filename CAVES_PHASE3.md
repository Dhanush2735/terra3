# Phase 3 — surveyed caves and mapped tunnels

Started: surveyed centerline reader and generator hook. No approved survey is bundled yet. Existing Phase 2 routes remain the fallback. Tunnel import has not started; do not rerun the finished Grottocenter crawl.

Pilot manifest `cave_surveys_v1.tsv`: entrance latitude and longitude in signed microdegrees (must match the existing public CaveData record), source/attribution, then semicolon-separated x,y,z offsets in blocks on Terra3's projected axes. Fields are tab-separated. One connected ordered route per matched entrance in this version. First station must be directly beneath the entrance (Y offset -64 to -10). All stations must be at least 10 blocks below entrance elevation, within the documented bounds and within the generated world's height. The generator interpolates the centerline and uses an approximate 2.4-block carve radius; surveyed cross-sections, branches, and accessible route checks are not included. Existing rock, surface, water and sensitive-coordinate checks still apply. Watch for `REALCAVE BLUEPRINT` only after a real row has been approved and imported.

Candidate: University of Bristol Spelaeological Society Burrington Area downloads (https://ubss.org.uk/research/cave_surveys/burrington/). Before import, verify the exact file's derivative redistribution terms and any required attribution, match a permitted entrance to CaveData, and convert the survey to the correct Terra3 projection and vertical reference. Reject protected coordinates and unanchored route data; do not ship fixtures as surveys. Test in fresh chunks before expanding to more caves.

### Tunnel carver pilot code

`MappedTunnelRoutes` reads offline-vetted absolute projected `x,y,z` stations (`provenance<TAB>x,y,z;x,y,z;...`) and indexes short legs to overlapping chunks, including negative coordinates. `TerraChunkGenerator` carves eligible buried rock along those legs with the existing land, roof and material guards. No raw OSM layer is interpreted as Y. This is a buried-alignment pilot only; there is no portal-opening, railway/road placement, or real route bundled. A verified 3D route and safe portal policy remain necessary before marking the mapped tunnel part complete. No full build or Minecraft test for this code.
